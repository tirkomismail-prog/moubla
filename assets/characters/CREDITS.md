# Model credits

`soldier.glb` and `textures/` are generated by `tools/characters/build_soldier.py`, `horse.glb` and
`textures/horse*` by `tools/characters/build_horse.py` (Blender, headless).
All third-party assets below are **CC0 1.0** (public domain) unless noted; the build scripts download them themselves.

- **Body, skeleton, skin weights and the helper shells used for the clothes**:
  MakeHuman / MPFB 2 (https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2).
- **Skin, eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes, hair**: MakeHuman system assets
  (https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html):
  skin `middleage_caucasian_male`, eyes `low-poly` with the `brownlight` texture, eyebrows `eyebrow001`,
  eyelashes `eyelashes01`, hair `short02`.
- **Unshaven skin**: `jartur69_middleage_slavic_male_with_genitals_and_beard` by jartur69, MakeHuman
  asset pack skins02 (http://www.makehumancommunity.org/node/1508).
- **Beard and moustache**: `rehmanpolanski_beard_viking` and `rehmanpolanski_moustache_viking` by
  Rehman Polanski, MakeHuman asset pack bodyparts05 (http://www.makehumancommunity.org/node/2614,
  http://www.makehumancommunity.org/node/2615).
- **Gloves**: `toigo_gloves_short` by MargaretToigo, MakeHuman asset pack gloves01
  (http://www.makehumancommunity.org/node/1852); refitted to the soldier, the cuffs pulled in under the
  sleeves and skinned like the wrist (`tools/characters/build_soldier.py`).
- **Hood**: `donitz_monk_robe_hood` by Donitz, MakeHuman asset pack suits02
  (https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/suits02.html; two files read out of the pack);
  simplified, made shallower and open for the face, its cape fitted over the garments (`tools/characters/hood.py`).
- **Cloth, leather and armour textures**: ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com): Fabric062 (wool), Fabric008
  (quilted gambeson), Leather014, Chainmail004, Metal038 (bright steel of the weapons), Metal052C (worn iron of
  the plate armour, helmets and chanfron), Metal039 (lamellar), and for the
  weapons and shields Wood060, Planks039, PaintedWood003; fur: Curly Teddy Natural from Poly Haven
  (https://polyhaven.com/a/curly_teddy_natural).
- **Motion capture, standing** (idle, idle2, idle3, idle4): Open Motion Project by ACCAD (Advanced
  Computing Center for the Arts and Design), The Ohio State University
  (https://accad.osu.edu/research/motion-lab/mocap-system-and-data), takes Male1_A1_Stand, Male1_A2_Sway,
  Male2_A1_Stand, Male2_A2_Sway, licensed under **CC BY 3.0 Unported**
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/); changed: retargeted onto the soldier's skeleton, trimmed
  and looped by `tools/characters/mocap.py`.
- **Motion capture** (walk, run, walking backwards): CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database
  (http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu), BVH conversion by Bruce Hahne. "The data used in this project was obtained
  from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217." Free for research
  and commercial use.
- Clothes and armour of each outfit, helmets, ambient occlusion, levels of detail, retargeting and
  looping: generated by the build scripts in this repository.
- **Horse** (body, mane and tail, eyes, skeleton, skin weights, coat and hair textures with the coat's normal
  and ambient occlusion maps): "Rigged Horse" on OpenGameArt (https://opengameart.org/content/rigged-horse),
  the horse by Lyndon Daniels from the Realtime Ranchers pack, rigged by ChadM, CC0.
- Tack (saddle, saddle cloth, stirrups, bridle, reins), caparison and chanfron, the smoothing, markings mask
  and levels of detail of the horse, and its gaits (posed in the game): generated by `build_horse.py` and
  `src/battle/horse.js`.
