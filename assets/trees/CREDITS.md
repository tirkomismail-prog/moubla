# Tree credits

`trees.glb` and `textures/` are generated by `tools/trees/build_trees.py` (Blender, headless).
The third-party assets below are **CC0 1.0** (public domain), from Poly Haven; the build script downloads them itself.

- **Broadleaf foliage and bark**: "Island Tree 02" (https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_02) by Rob Tuytel
  (scanning, processing) and Rico Cilliers (cleanup, processing): crops of its crown (leaves with their twigs)
  rendered into the foliage cards, its branch texture with the normal map as the bark.
- **Fir twigs and bark**: "Fir Tree 01" (https://polyhaven.com/a/fir_tree_01) by Rob Tuytel (photography)
  and Rico Cilliers (modeling): its twig cards put together into the fronds, its bark texture with the normal map.
- Trunks and branches, the arrangement of the crowns, levels of detail and impostors: generated by
  `build_trees.py`; the wind and the choice of the level of detail: `src/battle/trees.js`.
