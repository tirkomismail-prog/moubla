# Ground texture credits

The textures here are generated by `tools/ground/build_ground.py` from these Poly Haven and
ambientCG textures, all **CC0 1.0** (public domain); the script downloads them itself.

- **meadow**: "Grass 004" (https://ambientcg.com/a/Grass004) by ambientCG (Lennart Demes)
- **moss_ground**: "Ground 037" (https://ambientcg.com/a/Ground037) by ambientCG (Lennart Demes)
- **grass_twigs**: "Forest Ground 01" (https://polyhaven.com/a/forrest_ground_01) by Rob Tuytel
- **dry_grass**: "Grass Ground" (https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_ground) by Charlotte Baglioni
- **dirt**: "Forest Ground 04" (https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_ground_04) by Rob Tuytel, Rico Cilliers
- **needles**: "Forest Ground 03" (https://polyhaven.com/a/forrest_ground_03) by Rob Tuytel
- **cracked_earth**: "Dry Ground 01" (https://polyhaven.com/a/dry_ground_01) by Rob Tuytel
- **sand_gravel**: "Sandy Gravel 02" (https://polyhaven.com/a/sandy_gravel_02) by Dario Barresi
- **snow**: "Snow 02" (https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_02) by Rob Tuytel
- **trodden_snow**: "Snow 03" (https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_03) by Rob Tuytel
- **mossy_rock**: "Mossy Rock" (https://polyhaven.com/a/mossy_rock) by Rob Tuytel
- **rock**: "Rock Face" (https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face) by Greg Zaal, Dario Barresi

`grass*.webp` (`tools/ground/build_grass.py`): clumps of "Grass Medium 01"
(https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_01, Rob Tuytel and Rico Cilliers) and "Grass Medium 02"
(https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_02, Rico Cilliers), Poly Haven, CC0, rendered from the side.
